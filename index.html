I'm a STEM student at Ismailia STEM High School, based in Port Said, Egypt. My work sits at the intersection of three layers most people keep separate: embedded hardware, full-stack software, and data.

On the hardware side, I design and program embedded systems with Arduino as part of ongoing STEM research. On the software side, I build full-stack applications — React on the front end, Express on the back — and use Python, Pandas, and NumPy to analyze the data those systems generate. Cybersecurity training through Egypt's National Telecommunications Institute shapes how I think about all of it: not just whether a system works, but whether it holds up.

I'm currently interning as a Front-End AI Engineer at FlyRank AI, and actively looking for international scholarship and research opportunities where that combination is an asset rather than something I have to choose between.
